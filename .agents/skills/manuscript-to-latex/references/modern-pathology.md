# Modern Pathology adapter

Official Guide for Authors: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/modern-pathology/publish/guide-for-authors>

Profile last checked: 2026-09-15. Recheck immediately before a real submission.

## Research Article checks

- Main focus: a significant advance in human diagnostic pathology.
- Unstructured abstract: maximum 300 words.
- If the frozen source supplies Background, Methods, Results, and Conclusions subheadings, merge their text in that order into one continuous abstract paragraph in the derived package; keep `Keywords` on a separate line and record the transformation.
- Main body excluding abstract, legends, and references: maximum 7,000 words.
- Tables and figures combined: maximum 8.
- References: maximum 100.
- Title: no more than 150 characters; brief and informative; should not state a conclusion.
- American English only.
- Single-anonymized review.
- New submission may be a single Word or PDF file in a readable layout; editable Word or LaTeX source is required later. There is no strict initial formatting requirement.
- References may use any consistent style at initial submission, but the journal style is numerical/AMA-like and ordered by citation.

## Required content and checks

- corresponding author email and full postal address;
- author names and affiliations;
- competing-interest statement, including a statement when there are none;
- funding and sponsor role;
- author contributions and author approval;
- ethics approval date/reference number and informed consent statement for human research, or an author-confirmed exemption rationale;
- data-availability statement;
- generative-AI declaration when applicable;
- cover letter with corresponding-author contact, author approval, exclusivity, and relevant business relationships;
- editable tables, captions, and separately suitable artwork files as required by the current guide.

For first submission, prioritize a clear, stable, line-numbered PDF plus editable sources rather than imitating the final publisher typesetting. An explicit author request may disable line numbers or enable numbered headings for a preview; record the override in the package manifest and recheck the live portal requirements before submission.
