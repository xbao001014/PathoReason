# Journal of Pathology Informatics adapter

Official Guide for Authors: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-pathology-informatics/publish/guide-for-authors>

Profile last checked: 2026-09-15. Recheck immediately before a real submission.

## Research Article checks

- Article type: Research article — report of data from original research.
- Double-anonymized review, typically with at least two reviewers.
- Submit the title page and anonymized manuscript as separate files.
- Abstract: concise and factual, maximum 250 words.
- Keywords: 1–7, in English.
- Clearly defined and numbered sections; do not number the abstract.
- References use superscript Arabic numerals ordered by first appearance and an AMA-like list.
- Editable `.tex` is accepted; PDF alone is not an acceptable source file.
- British or American English is acceptable, but do not mix them.

## Separate or encouraged files

- Highlights are encouraged: a separate editable file with 3–5 bullets, each no more than 85 characters including spaces.
- A graphical abstract is encouraged: separate file, at least 531 × 1328 pixels (height × width) or proportionally larger, readable at 5 × 13 cm; preferred formats include TIFF, EPS, PDF, or Office files.
- Figures should normally be separate, logically named files. Text graphics may be embedded in LaTeX.
- The journal's Elsevier declaration tool produces a separate `.doc`/`.docx` competing-interest file; do not fabricate or replace that portal artifact.

## Anonymity boundary

The anonymous manuscript must not contain author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or identifying information in supplements. The title page should contain author information, corresponding-author address/email, acknowledgements, and the competing-interest declaration when it is not supplied separately. Inspect self-citations, repository links, filenames, PDF metadata, and acknowledgements manually; mechanical name removal is insufficient.

## Required content and checks

- current title page metadata and author order;
- author-confirmed ethics and consent statements for human data/material, including approval date and reference number where applicable;
- funding and sponsor role;
- data-availability statement (Option B encourages repository deposit/linking or an explanation of non-sharing);
- competing-interest form/status;
- generative-AI declaration when applicable;
- all citations and references mutually complete and real.
